Painting as a visible process

These speedpainting clips focus on the movement from rough composition to finished image. Instead of presenting only the final artwork, the videos make the sequence of decisions visible: blocking shapes, shifting color balance, refining anatomy, and deciding when to stop.

I use these pieces both as finished works and as process documents. They record hesitation, correction, and rhythm. That makes them useful for viewers who enjoy the image itself and for anyone interested in the mechanics of digital painting.
