Generated motion, edited intention

The AI work on the site is less about automation than about curation and direction. Prompting, iteration, image selection, and post-processing each shape the final result. What matters is not the tool by itself, but the visual language that emerges from repeated refinement.

These clips show two different sides of that process: one more abstract and process-driven, the other closer to a polished generated sequence. Together they frame AI art as a medium for experimentation rather than a shortcut to a finished image.
