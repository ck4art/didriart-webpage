A note on persona and image construction

Personalens can function as a study of how identity is staged through images. A portrait, mask, or figure is never only descriptive. It also organizes distance: what is revealed, what is stylized, and what remains withheld.

That makes this kind of work useful for thinking about self-presentation across media. The image is not just about a person. It is about the lens through which a person becomes legible.

As an article, this page can hold the longer reflection behind the image: references, artistic intent, and the balance between intimacy and construction.
